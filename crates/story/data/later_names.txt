# Names from after the lore cutoff of WoW Forever (GAMEPLAY.md 5.9, layer 4). This is the
# one list of later names. The pack builder drops each wiki paragraph that holds one, and
# every check of model text refuses each one: a narrator line, a saga, a tale, a summary,
# the history of a zone, a talk reply, and a /lore answer. A name that the player wrote
# first stays allowed (3.7).
#
# One name per line. The match ignores case and the marks between words, and matches whole
# words. The last word also counts at the start of a longer word: "Pandaria" holds
# "Pandarian", and "Blood Knight" holds "Blood Knights".
#
# Only a name that a chronicler of 25 ADP cannot know goes here: a person, place, order, or
# event of the later expansions. A name of 25 ADP whose wiki paragraphs go on to later
# stories, such as Gilneas or Varian, goes to `classic_names` in pack_sources.toml. A
# pattern of prose, such as "patch 1.12", goes to `terms` there.
#
