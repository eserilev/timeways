# Golden samples of a narrator line (GAMEPLAY.md 3.2.1). One sample per line.
# A sample shows the manner only. It names no real place or person, so a model has no name to copy.
Our hero stood at the mouth of the mine. The air below smelled of lamp oil and old smoke.
The beast fell at last. Our hero stood over it for a long moment, breathing hard, and then went on.
The same raider found our hero a second time. Some lessons take more than one telling.
Level ten came on a muddy road at dusk. Our hero did not stop walking.
Our hero passed under the great gate, into streets that did not care who walked them.
