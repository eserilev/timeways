# Golden samples of a narrator line (GAMEPLAY.md 3.2.1). The prompt shows each one as a
# pair: the moment and its lore first, then the line, so a model learns how a line uses them.
# A sample starts at "moment:". "who" and "lore" are optional. "hero" says how to name the
# hero: "$N", "the <race or class>", "by the title "<title>"", or "no name". A moment where
# the hero only arrived says "not at all, the line is about the place", and its line tells
# the place alone: the world is the main character.
# A sample names real places and people of 25 ADP, before Molten Core. The check refuses a
# line that copies 4 words in a row of a sample, and the log names a name in no fact.

moment: The player arrived in Tirisfal Glades. They had never been there before.
who: a Forsaken warlock
lore: Deathknell is a village of Tirisfal Glades. The plague killed its people, and the Scourge raised them. Now the Forsaken wake their own dead in its crypts.
hero: not at all, the line is about the place
line: Deathknell has twice buried its dead. The second time, they climbed back out.

moment: The player defeated Hogger. They never had before.
who: a human paladin
lore: Hogger leads the Riverpaw gnolls of Elwynn Forest. His raids on its farms go on, and the guards of Stormwind have a price on his head.
hero: the paladin
line: Hogger raided Elwynn's farms for years, and Stormwind's guards never stopped him. The paladin did.

moment: The player arrived in Westfall. They had never been there before.
lore: The stonemasons rebuilt Stormwind after the Second War. The House of Nobles refused to pay them, and they left as the Defias Brotherhood, which now holds Westfall.
hero: not at all, the line is about the place
line: Stormwind never paid the men who rebuilt it. In Westfall, their sons wear red masks.

moment: The player entered the dungeon The Deadmines. They had never been inside before.
lore: The Deadmines lie beneath Moonbrook in Westfall. There the Defias Brotherhood builds a ship with gold stolen from Stormwind.
hero: not at all, the line is about the place
line: Beneath Moonbrook, the Defias are building something with the gold they stole from Stormwind.

moment: The player defeated Edwin VanCleef. They never had before.
who: a human rogue

hero: the rogue
line: Edwin VanCleef asked Stormwind for his wages, and the kingdom never paid. The rogue settled the account instead.

line: The dwarves carved Ironforge from the mountain long before Stormwind laid its first stone. The Great Forge still burns at its heart.
