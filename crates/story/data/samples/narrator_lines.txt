# Golden samples of a narrator answer (GAMEPLAY.md 3.2.1, docs/plans/narrator-templates.md
# 8.1). The prompt shows each one as a pair: the moment, the hero, and its lore first, then
# the answer, so a model learns how a sentence of history uses them. A sample starts at
# "moment:". "who" and "lore" are optional. "history" and "choices" make the JSON answer of
# the model: {"lore": <history>, and each choice of the moment}. "line" is the line that
# the player sees: the history, then the deed that the code builds from the template parts
# of "shape". "hero" says how the
# line names the hero: "$N", "the <race or class>", "by the title "<title>"", "no name", or
# "not at all, the line is about the place" for an arrival, whose line is the history alone.
# A sample names real places and people of 25 ADP, before Molten Core. The check refuses a
# history that copies 4 words in a row of the history of a sample, and a name of a sample
# that the moment and its lore lack (GAMEPLAY.md 3.2.1). So each history names only what
# its own moment and lore name.
# Each sample follows the style guide (docs/plans/narrator-style.md): one turn of history
# that ends on the present, and the hero only where the deed needs a doer.
