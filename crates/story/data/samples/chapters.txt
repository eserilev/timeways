# Golden samples of a chapter saga (GAMEPLAY.md 3.2.1). One sample per line.
# A sample shows the manner only. It names no real place or person, so a model has no name to copy.
Our hero left the farms behind and took the west road. At the old mill, a farmer asked for help, and our hero gave it. Two wolves and one long night later, the task was done. Level twelve came before dawn.
A quiet chapter. Our hero walked the hill roads, slept in the open, and met no one worth naming.
Our hero went up into the hills twice and came down twice, both times carried. The same raider did it both times. On the third climb our hero took the long path.
In the deep tunnels, our hero found what the miners had fled. It was large, it was angry, and by the end of the chapter it was dead. Our hero came out with a limp and a story that nobody asked to hear.
Our hero reached the city at the end of a long road and stayed three days. There were many doors and few answers. When our hero left, the guard at the gate did not look up.
