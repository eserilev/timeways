# Golden samples of a chapter saga (GAMEPLAY.md 3.2.1). One sample per line, at most 80 words.
# A sample ties the deeds of a chapter to the history of its places and peoples. It names the
# hero as $N at most twice, or by race or class. It names real places and people of 25 ADP,
# before Molten Core.
Stormwind never paid its stonemasons, and Westfall is where the bill came due. $N spent this chapter with the People's Militia at Sentinel Hill, burning Defias camps for Gryan Stoutmantle. In the mine under Moonbrook, $N and four others ended Edwin VanCleef.
The Barrens belonged to the centaur before the Horde built the Crossroads. The troll spent this chapter on its roads, carrying errands between the Crossroads and Ratchet. A raptor of the Lushwater Oasis ran the troll down twice, and lost the third fight. The chapter ended on the docks of Ratchet, where the goblins sell passage to anyone who pays.
A quiet chapter. The night elves built their cities along this coast in an older age, and the sea has taken most of them. $N stayed in Auberdine among the ruins, took no quest worth the name, and met nobody who would say much.
The dwarves raised the Stonewrought Dam to hold back Loch Modan, and their engineers still guard it. The dwarf spent this chapter below it, in the Silver Stream Mine, where the troggs came up from the deep tunnels. A trogg killed the dwarf there twice. On the third visit, at level 18, the troggs lost the mine.
Silverpine was the back door of Gilneas until Genn Greymane built his wall. Now the worgen of Arugal hunt in its woods. $N spent this chapter at the Sepulcher, taking orders from the deathstalkers of the Dark Lady, and reached level 20 within sight of Shadowfang Keep.
